Game jam game I made in godot over 2 weeks with some friends for the 2024 Pirate Software Game Jam. I worked on the programming and implementation, and got help from some friends for all the required assets and playtesting. Give it a try! ## Write-up

This Game jam actually required game design document (GDD) to be submitted along with the game. The rest of this page is paraphrased from this document. If you'd like to view the full document, you can [view it here](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1ZpVd004_lcyOU76NwUiVP2P-cJ6U6r2j-s881cVQ1J0/).
## Concept

### Mechanics

#### Transmutation Cards

Allows the player to add or remove transmutations to themself. These transmutations give the player new abilities, such as double jumps, wall jumps, lifting boxes, and flipping levers.

<ProjectImage src={cardsGif} alt="Transmutation cards mechanic" caption="Player selecting from a hand of cards." maxWidth="medium" />

#### Box

Box that can be lifted and moved by the player with the "Lift" transmutation card enabled.

<ProjectImage src={boxGif} alt="Box lifting mechanic" caption="Player using the strength power-up to lift a box." maxWidth="medium" />

#### Toggleable Blocks

Blocks that can be toggled on and off through a pressure plate. When on, they collide with objects (player, box, creature). When inactive, these objects can go through the blocks.

<ProjectImage src={toggleGif} alt="Toggleable blocks mechanic" caption="Player toggleing a pressure plate by placing a box on it." maxWidth="medium" />

#### Moveable Platforms

Platforms that move between 2 locations depending on the value of a switched lever. Switch is controlled by the player with an equipped "Intelligence" card.

<ProjectImage src={leverGif} alt="Moveable platforms mechanic" caption="Player using the intelligence power-up to switch a lever." maxWidth="medium" />

#### Creature

Walking NPC that will kill the player on contact. Can walk on platforms and boxes, and will activate pressure plates. Walks straight until colliding with an object or reaching an edge, then turns around.

<ProjectImage src={creatureGif} alt="Creature AI mechanic" caption="Creature walking towards the player and killing them upon touching." maxWidth="medium" />


### Theme Interpretation (Shadows and Alchemy)

This game primarily focuses on the **transmutation** interpretation of the **Alchemy** theme. The player has the ability to change the abilities of the playable character to interact with the level. Future additions would introduce cards that implement transmutations to the **level** and general **physics** as well. The **Shadow** interpretation comes in the form of the art style, featuring a shadowy blob for the main character and NPC found in the game, along with a dark color palette and aesthetic. There were plans to introduce a secretive, shadowy enemy figure in the lore, but this did not make it through development.


### Visual and Audio Style

#### Visual Design

A somewhat monochrome palette with brightly colored intractable objects will make it visually clear to the player what options they have for each level. Semi minimalistic pixel art with distinct shapes and faces will give the game a "cute" feel. The player should be bouncy and very animated to get this across. Keep the objects simple and repeatable.

<ProjectImage src={runningGif} alt="Character running" caption="Player run animation. I'm proud of this one, look at the little dude go!" maxWidth="medium" />


#### Audio

Area for future improvement. As of now, we are using free commercially licensed assets for our audio rather than making our own. "Retro" sound effects for jumping, dying, etc. should be used to give a simple and almost nostalgic feel to the game. Similarly, the music should not be too distracting, and should feature minimalist and loopable songs that are not overly repetitive or intrusive to the gameplay.

### Game Concept

Platmorfer is a platformer puzzle game based on utilizing different cards to transmute the player/level in different ways to reach the end of each level.

### Inspirations

#### Toodee and Topdee

[Toodee and Topdee](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1303950/Toodee_and_Topdee/) is a very creative puzzle platformer game that allows the player to switch control between two unique characters, one inhabiting the 2D "platformer" world and the other inhabiting the 3D "top down" world. Changing how the player and environment functions during the level is how the player is meant to solve various puzzles, making the player think about the correct order to utilize each character's abilities in.

<ProjectImage src={toodeeImg} alt="Toodee and Topdee game" caption="TooDee and Topdee" maxWidth="medium" />

#### General Platformers

Games like Celeste, Super Meat Boy, Super Mario 64, etc. all feature similar platforming mechanics that we wanted to incorporate into our game. Having intuitive platformer controls along with familiar abilities such as wall jumping, double jumping, etc. will make our game easier to pick up and understand for many people.

### Player Experience

Each level will provide a new hand of cards, a defined number of limited slots, and a puzzling layout with a handful of environmental objects that need to be interacted with to make it to the end goal. The player will be required to use their cards in a precise, but not a strictly restricted, order.

### Target Audience

We are targeting semi-casual gamers who enjoy somewhat challenging platforming games, along with basic puzzle elements that require a moderate amount of effort. We do not want to alienate too many players by making the platforming or puzzling too difficult, but we do not want it to be so easy as to not propose any challenge whatsoever. Gamers familiar with at least a few mainstream puzzle or platforming games should find this game familiar and approachable.

### Unique Selling Point

The unique mechanic in this game is the ability to transmute the player character with given cards for each level. With a limited number of slots, the player must decide when each transmutation card is best used to complete the level. Replacing a card with a new card will cause that card to be lost for the remainder of the level, so using cards in a deliberate order will provide the main puzzle aspect of our game.

## Conclusion

Thanks for reading! This was a longer game jam, and I had a lot of fun being able to take my time with the game development process. Believe it or not, I actually had the most fun with the pixel art! I really like how the little player character turned out. I feel like I was able to get a lot of personality out of the few pixels I had!
